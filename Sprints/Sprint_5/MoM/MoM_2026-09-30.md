## Notes / Discussion
- **Sprint 4 demo:** The team showed the Android phone and Wear OS port (about 90% complete), streak tracking and goal progress rings on Android and Wear OS, and XP and levels on iOS.
- **Platform gap:** Gamification was split by platform at the end of Sprint 4 (streaks and goals on Android, XP and levels on iOS). The team will bring both platforms to the same feature set early in Sprint 5.
- **New feature – 1v1 matches:** The team proposed a 1v1 mode for two players who play against each other: friend another player, start a match, and track points, fouls, possession, and wins. Each player logs their own plays from the watch during the game, with a Red vs Blue scoreboard on top of the existing court design.
- **Sprint 5 goals:** Complete the Android port, bring gamification to parity across platforms, build the 1v1 feature, and run integration, system, and user testing.

## Decisions
- Sprint 5 will include platform parity for gamification and the new 1v1 match feature.
- The 1v1 feature will be built on iOS and watchOS first, then ported to Android and Wear OS.
- Owners: gamification parity and 1v1 iPhone/watch – Melvin; connecting two players' phones – Kaleb; Android/Wear OS port of 1v1 – Kellam.
