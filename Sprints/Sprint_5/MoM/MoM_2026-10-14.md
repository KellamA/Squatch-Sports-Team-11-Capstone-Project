## Notes / Discussion
- **Progress:** The team reported on the Sprint 5 work in progress: gamification parity between iOS and Android, and the design of the 1v1 match feature.
- **1v1 design:** The team walked through how a match works: friends list with win/loss records, match setup with street rules (1s and 2s or 2s and 3s, play to a target score, win by 2, make it take it), a Red vs Blue scoreboard on the court, and tracking points, fouls, turnovers, and possession changes such as losing the ball while dribbling.
- **Watch input:** Players have their watch on during a game, so plays should be logged from the Apple Watch and the phone should update automatically.
- **Two players:** To actually compete, each player needs to use their own phone. The team will connect two nearby iPhones for a shared match this sprint; online accounts can be considered later.
- **Testing:** The team will collect integration, system, and user testing evidence before the end of the sprint.

## Decisions
- The 1v1 feature keeps the existing court design and adds the Red vs Blue scoreboard on top.
- Watch scorekeeping is the most important part of the feature.
- Nearby phone-to-phone play is in scope for Sprint 5; online friends and accounts move to a later sprint.
