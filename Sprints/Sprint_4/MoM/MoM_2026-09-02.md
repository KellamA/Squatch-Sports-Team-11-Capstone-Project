## Notes / Discussion
- **Where we are:** The project was originally scoped as a two-semester iOS app. The team's iOS and watchOS features reached a level where they were brought into the production Squatch Sports app and released.
- **Android and watch expansion:** Port the existing iOS and watchOS work to Android, including an Android smartwatch (Wear OS) experience, using AI tools and Android Studio emulators to produce a working copy.
- **Stats and charts review:** Take a fresh look at how statistics and charts are presented. The question is not only "is the data correct" but "does the presentation pull the user back in." This review feeds directly into the gamification work.
- **Gamification:** Design and implement a gamification layer using Duolingo as the reference point: streaks, progress, small rewards, and gentle nudges that make repeated use feel worthwhile rather than obligatory.
- **"Hide the broccoli":** The app's core value (data entry, tracking, and reviewing performance) is the "broccoli" users need but won't naturally want to do. The goal is to wrap it in something enjoyable enough that logging workouts doesn't feel like homework. Gamification is the mechanism; hiding the broccoli is the goal.
- **AI usage vs. hand-coded work:** The team should use AI for most of the build, especially the port, boilerplate, and scaffolding. Some items from the stats/gamification list should be selected and implemented by the team, focusing on items that involve data manipulation, UI manipulation, and an animation or alert.
- **Feature ideas from the client:**
  - Streak: current and best
  - Goals: number of shots and percentage made (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly)
  - Time: minutes practiced per day, week, and month
  - Areas where more work is needed, with prompts to practice them
- **Watch focus:** Since most of the project so far has focused on watch development, the client suggested that showing stats, nudges, and gamification on the watch may fit the overall feel of the app.
- The client shared an `agents_baseline` project that structures project information and goals to improve AI tool performance.
- Recurring meetings every two weeks were discussed. The client is generally available on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday afternoons.

## Decisions
- Semester 2 focus: Android and Wear OS port, stats and chart review, and a gamification layer built around "hide the broccoli."
- AI tools will be used heavily for the port, boilerplate, and scaffolding. All AI-generated code will be reviewed and tested by the team.
- Client meetings will be held every two weeks over Microsoft Teams.
