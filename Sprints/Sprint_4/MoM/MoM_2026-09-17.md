## Agenda

## Notes / Discussion
- The client confirmed that the original iOS and watchOS work progressed far enough to be incorporated into the production Squatch Sports app and released. The second semester shifts from continuing the iOS implementation to expanding the app to Android and Wear OS while improving user engagement.
- The client asked the team to use AI heavily for the Android and Wear OS port, including project scaffolding, boilerplate, and platform conversion. The team will still review and test all AI-generated code.
- The client asked the team to review how statistics and charts are currently presented and whether they encourage users to come back to the app.
- Gamification is a major new goal. Using apps such as Duolingo as inspiration, the team will explore current and best streaks, shot goals, shooting-percentage goals, practice-time tracking, progress indicators, and prompts that point out areas needing more practice.
- The client repeated the "hide the broccoli" idea: logging workouts, entering data, and reviewing performance should feel enjoyable so players keep using the app.
- The client asked that some features be implemented primarily by the students rather than generated by AI. These features should combine data manipulation, UI manipulation, and an animation or alert.
- The team presented its sprint plan:
  - **Sprint 4 (Aug 27 – Sep 30):** two gamification features, Android port to about 90%, and Wear OS integration
  - **Sprint 5 (Oct 1 – Oct 31):** complete the port, improve stats and charts, integrate and refine gamification, and run integration and defect testing
  - **Sprint 6 (Nov 1 – Dec 5):** final regression testing, UI/UX polish, technical documentation and handoff, and the final client demo
- Risks discussed: the team's limited Android and Wear OS experience, keeping feature parity between the Apple and Android versions, and phone-to-watch communication on Wear OS.

## Decisions
- The Sprint 4, 5, and 6 deliverables were accepted as presented.
- Team members may build gamification features on the platform that best fits each feature (Android phone, Wear OS, or iOS).
- Sprint 4 owners: streak tracking – Melvin; goal progress – Kaleb and Melvin; Android port and Wear OS integration – Kellam with team support.
- Each sprint will be demonstrated to the client at the bi-weekly Microsoft Teams meeting, and client feedback will be used to plan the following sprint.
